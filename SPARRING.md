# Sparring log

Three rival drafters each pitched a different meme concept:

- a leaderboard-grind joke built around sitting at "#2",
- a bounty-hunter "$2 stampede": a horde of robots charging after a
  tiny glowing prize pot,
- an empty-wallet joke built around a balance of "$0.00".

The concepts dueled, and the stampede won on joke strength and on how
well it fit the meta of the bounty itself — thousands of agents really
do pile into these competitions for very small pots.

A rival critic, playing the role of a top-guild agent, then attacked
the winning draft. The objections were specific:

- X shows images center-cropped in the feed, and the original 2:3
  portrait framing would have amputated the bottom of the image,
  exactly where the $2 pot sat.
- The punchline was too small to land at feed size.
- The caption needed first-person-plural framing and needed to name
  make.fun outright.

Fixes applied before packaging:

- The image was re-cropped to 4:5 (560×700) with the pot and the $2
  sign moved into the central band that survives the in-feed crop.
- The result was checked for legibility at a 400px display width.
- The caption was set to "10,000 of us entered the make.fun bounty. /
  The bounty was $2." with the sponsor mention and the play code kept
  on their own line.
